Where To Go in Memphis

Curated spots for the crew. Local gems and fast-food classics - all worthy of the Biscuit Board.

🏠 Local Memphis Spots

Brother Juniper's
Local

📍 3519 Walker Ave, Midtown

Memphis breakfast institution. House-made biscuits are thick, buttery, and legendary. Don't skip the gravy option.

Sunrise Memphis
Local

📍 670 Jefferson Ave, Downtown

Farm-to-table breakfast done right. Creative chicken biscuit with rotating seasonal ingredients and serious presentation.

The Arcade Restaurant
Local

📍 540 S Main St, South Main

Memphis' oldest diner (est. 1919). Classic Southern biscuits with old-school charm. Historic atmosphere adds to presentation.

Blue Plate Cafe
Local

📍 5469 Poplar Ave (+ other locations)

Home-style Southern cooking with big fluffy biscuits. Rotating daily specials. A great all-around score candidate.

Elwood's Shack
Local

📍 4523 Summer Ave, East Memphis

BBQ joint that does a mean Southern breakfast. Smoky bold flavors - expect high Taste scores and creative Chicken prep.

Miss Polly's Soul City Cafe
Local

📍 154 Beale St, Downtown

Soul food on Beale Street. Crispy fried chicken on a fluffy Southern biscuit. Pure Memphis energy.

Jim's Place
Local

📍 518 Perkins Ext, East Memphis

Memphis institution since 1921. Greek-owned Southern cooking - the biscuits are quietly excellent.

Cracker Barrel
Local

📍 Multiple Memphis locations

Butter-soaked biscuits every time. A reliable baseline - good for calibrating your rating scale early in the season.

🍔 Fast Food Chains

Chick-fil-A
Fast Food

🍗 Chicken Biscuit

The gold standard. Perfectly seasoned chicken fillet on a buttery biscuit. The benchmark every other biscuit gets judged against.

Hardee's
Fast Food

🍗 Chicken Fillet Biscuit

Made-from-scratch biscuits - flaky, layered, and tall. May have the best biscuit in all of fast food.

Popeyes
Fast Food

🍗 Chicken & Biscuit combo

World-class chicken. Dense buttery biscuit. Pair the spicy tender with the biscuit for a potential 5-star Taste experience.

McDonald's
Fast Food

🍗 McChicken Biscuit

Soft, pillowy biscuit with a crispy chicken patty. Simple and consistent. A solid mid-range fast food baseline.

Burger King
Fast Food

🍗 Chicken Biscuit

Underrated breakfast option. Solid chicken with a decent biscuit. Won't blow your mind but consistently delivers.

KFC
Fast Food

🍗 Chicken & Biscuit combo

The Colonel's original. Biscuit is soft and cakey - an interesting Texture rating for the board.

Waffle House
Fast Food

🍗 Chicken Biscuit

Southern soul in its purest form. When it's good, it's GREAT. The vibe alone adds points to Presentation.

Starbucks
Fast Food

🍗 Chicken, Maple Butter & Egg on Buttermilk Biscuit

Bold wildcard. Maple-honey butter is unexpected. Clean presentation. Great for adventurous raters.
